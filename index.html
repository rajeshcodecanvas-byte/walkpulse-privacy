Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to WalkPulse. We value your trust and prioritize your privacy above all else. This privacy statement outlines our strict adherence to data protection principles regarding your movement metrics.

1. Privacy-First Architecture & Data Collection

WalkPulse functions as a Privacy-First, offline-only application. We design our tracking systems to process data transparently:

  • Physical Sensor Access: WalkPulse utilizes your device's built-in step counter sensor (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) to compute your metrics locally.
  • Zero Cloud Syncing: No step counts, location metrics, or health data points are uploaded, synchronized, or monitored on external servers. All information remains locked safely inside your device's offline data structures.

2. Android Permissions Handled

To deliver accurate real-time pedometer tracking, our application requests explicit runtime access to:

  • Physical Activity Recognition: Required to query underlying step hardware metrics safely.
  • Post Notifications: Vital to maintaining a transparent Android Foreground Service so you always know when tracking is live.

3. Third-Party Libraries & Services

Our baseline application processes data natively. If any third-party frameworks or monetization SDKs are introduced in public updates, they operate in sandboxed runtimes and do not access personal device tracking data.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our application processes no personal data, it does not knowingly gather, store, or target identifiable tracking metrics from children under the age of 13.
