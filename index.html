Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to WalkPulse. We value your trust and prioritize your privacy above all else. WalkPulse is engineered to provide professional-grade step tracking with an absolute commitment to data sovereignty.

1. Privacy-First Architecture

WalkPulse functions as a Privacy-First, 100% Offline application. Our core philosophy is that your health data belongs to you, and only you.

  • Physical Sensor Access: WalkPulse utilizes your device's built-in step counter hardware (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) to compute metrics. This processing happens entirely on-device.
  • Zero Cloud Syncing: No step counts, historical charts, BMI calculations, or weight records are ever uploaded to external servers. We do not maintain any cloud databases or monitoring systems.

2. Android Permissions & Transparency

To comply with Google Play's standard for transparency, we disclose the following permission usages:

  • Activity Recognition: Used to access hardware sensors for real-time step counting.
  • Foreground Service (Health): Required to ensure accurate tracking continues while the app is in the background. A persistent notification is shown to maintain user awareness.
  • Exact Alarms: Used specifically for the "Hydration Network" feature to trigger precise water intake reminders at user-defined intervals.
  • Post Notifications: Used to display live tracking progress and hydration alerts.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data (user profile, step history, weight records) is stored locally using secure Android internal storage. If you uninstall the application or use the "Factory Reset" feature within the app, all tracking data is permanently purged from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

WalkPulse does not knowingly collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII). It is safe for all users, including those under the age of 13, as no data leaves the physical device.
