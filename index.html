Privacy Policy

WalkPulse: Pedometer & Steps

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to WalkPulse: Pedometer & Steps, a pedometer, activity tracking, fitness analytics and hydration application developed by The CodeCanvas Studio.

WalkPulse is designed around a local-first privacy approach. The application does not require you to create a WalkPulse account, register an email address, or sign in to use its core tracking features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WalkPulse processes, how it is used, how it is stored, how Android permissions are used, and how you can control or delete information associated with the application.

2. Our Local-First Privacy Approach

WalkPulse is designed to process and store core activity, fitness, hydration and application information locally on your Android device whenever possible.

  • No WalkPulse account: WalkPulse does not require registration, login, or an email address for its core functionality.
  • No WalkPulse cloud account: WalkPulse does not operate a personal cloud account where your activity history is uploaded for storage.
  • Local processing: Core calculations such as step progress, distance, estimated calories, BMI and hydration progress are processed within the application.
  • Local storage: WalkPulse uses Android application storage for applicable local records and settings.

Important: Optional integrations such as Google Health Connect and Wear OS synchronization can exchange supported information when you enable those features. These platform integrations are different from uploading your information to a WalkPulse-operated cloud server.

3. Information WalkPulse Processes

Depending on the features you use, WalkPulse may process the following categories of information.

🚶 Activity Information

  • Step counts
  • Walking distance
  • Daily activity progress
  • Historical step records
  • Activity goals
  • Streak and achievement progress

⚖️ Fitness Profile Information

  • Height
  • Weight
  • Gender, when provided
  • Stride length

These values may be used to personalize calculations such as BMI, estimated calorie expenditure and walking distance.

💧 Hydration Information

  • Water intake amounts
  • Daily hydration goals
  • Water-entry timestamps
  • Hydration reminder preferences

⚙️ Application Preferences

  • Theme preferences
  • Activity goals
  • Notification preferences
  • Hydration reminder settings
  • Other settings required for application functionality

4. How WalkPulse Uses Information

WalkPulse uses information to provide the features that you choose to use within the application.

  • Track and display daily steps
  • Calculate and display walking distance
  • Provide estimated calorie information
  • Display daily, weekly, monthly and yearly activity trends
  • Provide BMI calculations
  • Maintain local weight history
  • Track water intake and hydration progress
  • Schedule hydration reminders
  • Track goals, achievements and streaks
  • Synchronize supported step information with Wear OS
  • Read and write supported step information through Google Health Connect when authorized
  • Display step progress through the Android home-screen widget

WalkPulse does not use your health or activity information for targeted advertising.

5. Step Tracking & Background Operation

WalkPulse uses Android's step counter sensor to track steps on compatible devices.

The application can use a health-type foreground service to maintain step tracking while the application is running in the background. Android may display an ongoing notification while this service is active.

WalkPulse does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for its core pedometer functionality.

Step-counting availability and accuracy can vary by device hardware, Android version, operating-system restrictions, battery-management settings and sensor behavior.

6. Android Permissions

WalkPulse requests Android permissions only for functionality that requires them.

🏃 Activity Recognition

ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION is used to support activity recognition and step-counting functionality on supported Android versions.

🔄 Foreground Service

FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH support the health-related foreground service used for ongoing step tracking when the application operates in the background.

🔔 Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS is used to display supported activity and hydration notifications on Android versions that require notification permission.

⏰ Exact Alarms

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM supports scheduled hydration reminders when exact reminder timing is enabled and permitted by Android.

Permission behavior can vary according to Android version, device manufacturer and user-controlled system settings.

7. Google Health Connect

WalkPulse optionally integrates with Android Health Connect.

Based on the permissions implemented by WalkPulse, the application requests permission to read and write step records through Health Connect.

📖 Reading Step Data

When you grant permission, WalkPulse can read StepsRecord information from Health Connect for the relevant time period used by the application.

This allows WalkPulse to retrieve supported step information and incorporate it into the application's activity experience.

✍️ Writing Step Data

WalkPulse requests permission to write StepsRecord information to Health Connect. This permission supports synchronization of supported step information with the Android health ecosystem.

🔐 Health Connect Privacy

WalkPulse does not sell Health Connect data.

Health Connect information is used only to provide the health and fitness functionality for which you grant permission.

WalkPulse does not use Health Connect step information for targeted advertising or unrelated purposes.

Managing Health Connect Permissions

You can review or revoke WalkPulse's Health Connect permissions through Android Health Connect settings. You can also manage or delete applicable Health Connect records using the controls provided by Android.

Health Connect is a separate Android platform service. Information stored in Health Connect is governed by the controls and privacy practices applicable to Health Connect and other applications connected to it.

8. Wear OS Synchronization

WalkPulse includes optional Wear OS functionality for compatible smartwatches.

The Wear OS component uses the Android Wearable Data Layer to synchronize supported step information between the smartwatch and the phone application.

⌚ Information Sent from the Watch

The Wear OS implementation sends:

  • Step count
  • Timestamp associated with the step update

The phone application receives the synchronized step count and uses it to update the live activity display.

Wear OS synchronization is used only to provide the requested cross-device activity-tracking functionality.

If you do not use the Wear OS functionality, the standard phone-based tracking experience does not require watch synchronization.

9. Local Database & Storage

WalkPulse uses a local Android database to maintain applicable activity and fitness history.

The application's local repository includes step-history records and weight-history records used to provide historical activity and fitness features.

This local information is used by WalkPulse to provide features such as historical charts, progress tracking, weight timelines and activity summaries.

WalkPulse does not require a WalkPulse-operated cloud database to store this local application history.

10. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

WalkPulse does not operate a personal cloud service for uploading and storing your activity history.

WalkPulse does not sell your health, fitness or activity information.

WalkPulse may exchange supported information with Android platform services and compatible devices when you explicitly enable functionality such as Google Health Connect or Wear OS synchronization.

These integrations are used to provide the corresponding features and operate according to the permissions and controls provided by Android and the connected platform.

Based on the application's current implementation, WalkPulse does not include an advertising SDK or an analytics service for collecting your activity data for advertising or behavioral analytics.

Third-party platform services such as Google Health Connect and Google Play services may process information according to their own terms and privacy practices. WalkPulse does not control the independent privacy practices of those platform services.

11. Data Retention

WalkPulse may retain locally stored information for as long as needed to provide features such as historical step tracking, weight history, hydration progress, goals, achievements and activity analytics.

Because applicable WalkPulse records are stored locally, they remain on your device until you delete them through available in-app controls, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Information stored separately by Health Connect is controlled separately through the Health Connect system.

12. Data Deletion

🗑️ WalkPulse Local Data

You can remove locally stored WalkPulse information using the application's available reset or data-deletion controls.

You may also use Android's application settings to clear WalkPulse application data or uninstall the application. Android normally removes the application's private local storage when the application is uninstalled.

🩺 Health Connect Data

Health Connect information is maintained separately by the Health Connect platform. Revoking WalkPulse's permission prevents further access according to the permission state, while applicable Health Connect records can be managed or deleted through Health Connect.

Removing WalkPulse's local application data does not automatically guarantee deletion of information that exists separately in Health Connect or another connected application.

13. Security

WalkPulse is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of personal activity information.

Local application information benefits from Android's application sandboxing, permission system and operating-system security mechanisms.

WalkPulse release builds use code shrinking, optimization and obfuscation to help protect the application code and reduce unnecessary resources.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the information handled by the application.
