Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Welcome to WalkPulse, a professional-grade pedometer and health analytics engine. This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising commitment to your data sovereignty and privacy.

1. The Privacy-First Architecture

WalkPulse is engineered as a 100% Offline Application. We believe that your health metrics are private and should never leave your physical device.

  • No Cloud Infrastructure: We do not operate servers, cloud databases, or external monitoring systems.
  • Local Processing Only: All step calculations, BMI analysis, and hydration logs are processed and stored locally on your device.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not require account creation, email registration, or social media logins.

2. Mandatory Permission Disclosures

To provide high-fidelity tracking, WalkPulse requires specific Android permissions. We use these exclusively for local functionality:

  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION: Accesses your device's hardware step-counter sensor to detect movement.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH: Enables the app to maintain tracking accuracy while in the background. A persistent notification is displayed to ensure user transparency.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display your live step count and hydration alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Powering the "Hydration Network" to trigger precise water intake reminders at your requested intervals.

3. Data Collection & Usage

While the app handles data, it does so in a "Blind" capacity. We handle the following categories of information solely within the app's local memory:

  • Physical Metrics: Height, Weight, and Gender (used to calculate BMI and Calorie expenditure).
  • Activity Logs: Daily step counts, distance covered, and active minutes.
  • Hydration Data: Timestamps of water intake and daily volume totals.

*None of this data is transmitted to The CodeCanvas Studio or any third party.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data. All records are stored in the app's private sandbox. If you wish to delete your data, you may:

  1. Use the "Factory Reset" feature within the app Settings to purge all local databases.
  2. Uninstall the application, which triggers the Android OS to automatically delete all associated local storage.

5. Children’s Privacy

WalkPulse is compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since the app collects Zero Personal Data and has no online component, it is safe for use by individuals of all ages, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

As WalkPulse evolves, we may update this policy. However, our core philosophy—that your data stays on your device—will remain unchanged.
